About Whenua Printing

Bring your ideas to life,
with limited spending &

Whenua Printing helps businesses bring campaign ideas to life with business cards, flyers, posters, name badges and pull-up banners. We aim to offer quality printing at prices more than 50% below comparable market prices.

Every order receives personal attention. Share your idea or requirements with us, and we’ll help organise the details, gather supplier quotes and coordinate your order from start to finish. Our delivery timeframe is 7–14 days, making us a good fit for campaigns and projects planned ahead.

Students starting a business or working on a project can also receive a discount with a valid student ID. We are Eco-friendly 🌳 mindful of printing’s environmental impact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our design and printing services,
artwork requirements, ordering and delivery.

What can I order?

We offer business cards, flyers, posters, staff name badges and pull-up banners. We also welcome custom enquiries for branded merchandise and corporate gifts featuring your company logo.

How do I get a quote?

Tell us the product, size, quantity, delivery postcode and date you need your order. Include your artwork if you have it ready. You can enquire through our website or by email, and we’ll help confirm the details.

Are the website prices final?

Our website prices are for reference only. Your final quote depends on your specifications, quantity and delivery requirements. All prices are in NZD, and your quote will clearly show any applicable GST and delivery charges before you confirm your order.

Can I save by ordering more?

Larger quantities or combined orders may help reduce the cost per item. Ask us to quote a few quantity options so you can choose what works best for your budget.

How long will my order take?

Production and delivery times vary by product, quantity, artwork and destination. We’ll confirm the estimated timeframe in your quote. If you have an event or deadline, please tell us before ordering.

Do you offer delivery?

We’ll confirm delivery options and charges based on your address and order. You can also ask about personal delivery when requesting your quote.

What if my artwork isn’t ready?

Send us what you have and tell us what you need help with. We’ll explain the file requirements, available assistance and any additional costs before proceeding. Your artwork and order details will be confirmed with you before printing.

What should I check before approving my artwork?

Please check all wording, names, contact details, sizes and layout carefully before approving your artwork. If anything needs changing, let us know before printing begins.

Can I make changes after placing my order?

Please contact us as soon as possible. We’ll check the progress of your order and confirm whether changes are still possible, along with any effect on the price or delivery timeframe.

What if there’s a problem with my order?

Please contact us with your order details, a description of the issue and clear photos where helpful. We’ll review what happened and work with you on an appropriate solution.
