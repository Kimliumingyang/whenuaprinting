3. Price Beat Promise

3.1 Eligible products

Our Price Beat Promise applies to standard business cards, flyers and pull-up banners within the product specifications and quantities we currently offer.

It does not automatically apply to other products or services.

Design, copywriting, installation, rush services, branded merchandise, corporate gifts and non-standard projects are outside this promise unless we expressly include them in writing before you order.

You must submit your request before accepting our quote, confirming your order or making payment for that order.

3.3 Eligible competitor offers

The competitor must be a genuine printing business operating in New Zealand and able to supply the quoted order to the required New Zealand delivery address.

Their offer must be:

  • A genuine, current written quote or verifiable firm offer.
  • Available for you to accept when you submit your request.
  • For the same eligible products and quantity.
  • Based on matching specifications and objectively comparable quality.
  • Able to meet the same required delivery deadline.
  • Based on equivalent payment and purchasing conditions.

Relevant specifications include dimensions, paper or material grade, paper weight, printed sides, colour requirements, finishing, lamination and any included stands, hardware or accessories.

Similar product names alone do not establish a valid comparison.

3.4 How prices are compared

We compare the total amount payable for the equivalent eligible order, including:

  • The printing and product price.
  • Any applicable GST.
  • Setup and mandatory production charges.
  • Required hardware or accessories.
  • Delivery to the same destination.
  • Any other unavoidable charges.

A lower printing price that results in a higher total payable does not qualify.

Prices for different quantities, materially different specifications or different delivery requirements must be adjusted to a genuinely comparable basis before a comparison can be made.

Where an offer includes additional products or services, we may reasonably require an itemised quote to identify the comparable eligible order.

Please email Whenuaprinting@gmail.com with the competitor’s quote, offer details or supporting link.

The information must allow us to identify:

  • The competitor.
  • The offer date and validity.
  • The product specifications and quantity.
  • The total payable and included charges.
  • The payment and delivery conditions.

You may redact unrelated personal or confidential information, provided the offer remains verifiable.

We may request clarification reasonably necessary to assess eligibility. If the information remains insufficient, we will explain what is missing rather than treating the offer as verified.

  • Fabricated, materially altered or fictitious quotes.

We will assess requests against these stated conditions. If a request does not qualify, we will explain the relevant reason.

3.7 The price-beaten quote

If a qualifying competitor’s total is lower than our quoted total, we will issue a written quote with a lower total price.

The promise provides a lower total quote. It does not offer a fixed percentage reduction unless we expressly advertise one.

If our quoted total is already lower than the qualifying competitor’s total, no further reduction is required under this promise.

Price beating applies to the comparable order total. Discounts are not multiplied by the number of items or added together across successive comparisons.

Other discounts, vouchers or promotions are not automatically added on top of a price-beaten quote.

The revised quote will state its validity period. Changes to the specifications, quantity or delivery requirements require reassessment and may result in a new quote.
